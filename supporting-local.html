FOR LOCAL BUSINESS OWNERS

How the initiative works

Supporting Local is a free community initiative connected to Daniel Smith's Around the Suburbs property market updates.

01. Display a posterPlace an A4 Around the Suburbs QR poster in your business window or another suitable spot.
02. Be featured for freeParticipating businesses can be featured in the relevant category here, with their business details and a link.
03. Share your storyWith your permission, we may take a photo together and introduce your business on social media, tagging your page.

There's no fee to participate and no obligation to refer clients. Interested in taking part? Get in touch with Daniel.

Business features and photographs are published with permission. Inclusion is for community visibility and does not imply endorsement of products or services.
