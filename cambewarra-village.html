CAMBEWARRA VILLAGE • 28 OCTOBER 2026

Village living
on the doorstep of major growth.

An established village at the foothills of Cambewarra Mountain, sitting beside one of the Nowra-Bomaderry area's major residential growth corridors.

By Daniel SmithRay White Shoalhaven Central Group

Sitting at the foothills of Cambewarra Mountain, Cambewarra Village has a very different feel to many of the residential areas surrounding Nowra and Bomaderry. Leafy streets, established homes and larger blocks give the village its own identity, while Nowra, Bomaderry and Berry remain within easy reach.

What makes Cambewarra Village particularly interesting right now is the scale of change occurring around it. The village itself remains established, but major residential growth is progressing nearby through the Moss Vale Road Urban Release Area.

CAMBEWARRA VILLAGE MARKET SNAPSHOT$1,147,500

Median house price, with 20 house sales in the past 12 months and a median 56 days on market. Indicative rental yield: 4.0%.

A small market where context matters

Cambewarra Village is a relatively tightly held residential market. With only a limited number of houses changing hands, the headline median can move noticeably depending on the mix of properties sold during a particular period.

That matters here because properties can vary considerably in land size, accommodation, age, outlook, presentation and improvements. A suburb-wide figure is useful context, but it should not be treated as a valuation of an individual home.

Living in Cambewarra Village

The village combines a semi-rural feel with convenient access to the larger centres of Nowra and Bomaderry. Cambewarra Public School has been part of the local community for more than 160 years, reinforcing the long-established village identity that distinguishes the area from newer residential estates.

Growth on the doorstep

The Moss Vale Road Urban Release Area is located between Bomaderry and Cambewarra Village and is one of the significant residential growth areas within the wider Nowra-Bomaderry region.

Shoalhaven City Council currently identifies a projected development yield of approximately 3,400 residential lots across the Urban Release Area. Major water and wastewater infrastructure needed to service the future development was completed in March 2025 and is operational.

The scale of that planned growth means the wider area surrounding Cambewarra Village is likely to look very different over the longer term, with more homes, roads, open space and supporting infrastructure progressively added nearby.

Why the distinction matters

It is important not to treat Cambewarra Village and the surrounding new growth areas as though they are one identical property market.

The established village has its own housing stock, block sizes, streetscape and buyer appeal. As new residential communities develop nearby, that contrast may become an increasingly important part of how buyers view the village.

Daniel’s take

Cambewarra Village is one of those locations where I would not put too much weight on the median price alone. With a small number of annual sales, the individual characteristics of a property become particularly important.

What I find interesting is what happens as thousands of new homes are progressively added around the village. Cambewarra Village will increasingly sit beside a much larger residential population while continuing to offer something quite different itself.

For existing homeowners, the more useful question is not simply what the suburb median is. It is where your particular property sits against the homes buyers are currently comparing it with.
