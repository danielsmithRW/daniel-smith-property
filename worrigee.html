Worrigee has changed considerably over the years, but its appeal has remained fairly consistent: established family neighbourhoods, local shopping, and a mix of residential homes and acreage, all within easy reach of Nowra.

The next chapter is already taking shape.

Planning approval has been granted for the new Worrigee Public School and Public Preschool. Construction of the preschool is underway, while the primary school project is progressing through its construction programme. The preschool is forecast for completion in 2027, followed by the primary school in 2028.

The school project also includes planned improvements to Booligal Road, pedestrian access and drop-off facilities. These changes matter to residents as the suburb grows.

Infrastructure is changing too

The planned intersection upgrade at Worrigee Road, Greenwell Point Road and Millbank Road includes a roundabout and pedestrian improvements. The school infrastructure programme identifies completion of this intersection upgrade by late 2027, ahead of the primary school's opening.

The suburb-wide median only tells part of the story.

Different homes can perform very differently within the same suburb. Bedrooms, land size, condition, layout and buyer appeal can all affect the result.

That difference shows why bedrooms, land, condition, layout and buyer appeal all need to be considered, not just the suburb headline.

What do interest rates mean for Worrigee homeowners?

Interest rate changes can affect mortgage repayments, buyer borrowing capacity and household budgets. But a change in rates does not automatically mean every property will rise or fall in value. Local supply, comparable sales, condition and buyer demand all matter.

What could your property be worth today?

The price you paid, an online estimate and what a buyer may pay in today's market can be different figures. If you haven't had your home appraised since buying it, a current assessment can provide useful context for refinancing, renovating or future planning.
