AROUND THE SUBURBS • WORRIGEE

Growing, changing and still
unmistakably Worrigee.

By Daniel SmithRay White Shoalhaven Central Group

Worrigee has changed considerably over the years, but its appeal has remained fairly consistent: established family neighbourhoods, local shopping, and a mix of residential homes and acreage, all within easy reach of Nowra.

The next chapter is already taking shape.

Planning approval has been granted for the new Worrigee Public School and Public Preschool. Construction of the preschool is underway, while work on the primary school is scheduled to begin in October 2026. The preschool is forecast for completion in 2027, followed by the primary school in 2028.

Locals can also currently have their say on the new school’s values, uniform and logo, with the community survey open until 4 October 2026.

Infrastructure is changing too

Council is evaluating construction tenders for the new roundabout at Worrigee Road, Greenwell Point Road and Millbank Road. Construction is currently estimated to run from February to August 2027, with improved pedestrian and cycling access included in the design.

Median house sale price based on 67 sales.

The suburb-wide median only tells part of the story.

Three-bedroom houses recorded a median of $760,000 and a median 27 days on market. Four-bedroom houses recorded a median of $850,000 but took 54 days.

That difference shows why bedrooms, land, condition, layout and buyer appeal all need to be considered, not just the suburb headline.

The median house rent was $620 per week over the same 12-month period, with 93 houses leased.
