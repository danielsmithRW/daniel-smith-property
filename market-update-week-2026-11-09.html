What caught my attention

Bomaderry led the week's new listings, with six properties in the Pricefinder for-sale snapshot and one recorded sale. Nowra recorded five new for-sale entries and two sales, the highest sales count among the six suburbs.

South Nowra represented 127 of the 307 REA advertisements, around 41% of the total. This is advertised stock, not a count of established homes. House-and-land packages and other property types can influence the figure, so it should not be read as direct competition for every established property.

Worrigee and South Nowra recorded no sales in the supplied weekly sold snapshot. A single week does not establish a longer-term trend; the value will come from comparing these figures consistently over time.

How to read these figures

REA advertised stock is a snapshot of total listings shown for each suburb, including property types that cannot be reliably separated from house-and-land packages using the available filters. FS means the Pricefinder weekly 'For Sale' snapshot; JS means its weekly 'Just Sold' snapshot. The 15 FS entries include a Nowra commercial listing. These are distinct measures from different sources and should not be subtracted from one another to infer net stock change.

Sources: realestate.com.au suburb listing counts supplied 16 November 2026; Pricefinder weekly FS and JS snapshots for 9–15 November 2026, supplied by Daniel Smith. Figures are a reporting snapshot and may change as listings and sales are updated.
