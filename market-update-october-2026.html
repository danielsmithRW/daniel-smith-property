What changed towards the end?

The final three snapshots recorded 29, 45 and 56 buyers met, across 38, 39 and 36 opens respectively. Calculated attendance per open increased from 0.76 to 1.15, then 1.56.

For me, that is the encouraging part of these numbers. More people came through the door in the latest two snapshots, even though the number of opens did not increase. Whether that continues, and whether it leads to more offers and exchanges, is what I will be watching next.

The five reports also recorded 26 leases signed. Conditional sales, exchanges and settlements are separate campaign stages and may involve the same property, so they should not be added together as distinct sales.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN ON THE GROUND

Attendance is a start.
Follow-through matters.

My read is that the recent lift in office attendance deserves attention, without getting ahead of what it tells us. An inspection gives a buyer the opportunity to act. Feedback, a second inspection, an offer and an exchange tell us more about their intention.

For sellers, I would look closely at the competing properties a buyer can choose from and how the campaign is converting interest into inspections and offers. The 280 advertised listings cover very different property types and price points, so the relevant competition will be much narrower for any individual home.
