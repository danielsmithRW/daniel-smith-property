What this means for the local market

The Shoalhaven is not one uniform property market. A three-bedroom home in Nowra, a family residence in North Nowra and a house-and-land package in South Nowra can attract different buyers, even at overlapping prices. Median figures provide context, but recent comparable sales, condition, land characteristics and buyer demand are more useful when assessing an individual property.
