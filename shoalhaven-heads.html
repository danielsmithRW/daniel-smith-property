PROPERTY MARKET SNAPSHOT

SHOALHAVEN HEADS MARKET SNAPSHOT • 25 NOVEMBER 2026

57 recorded sales from October 2025 to September 2026, 0% annual growth, 148 median days on market and 35 properties currently advertised.

Reported median$1.1M

Where the river meets the sea, life moves at a slightly different pace. Nestled between the Shoalhaven River, Seven Mile Beach and the foothills of Coolangatta Mountain, Shoalhaven Heads captures much of what makes the South Coast special.

A lifestyle shaped by the river and ocean

Whether it's an early morning beach walk, an afternoon fishing on the river, a round of golf, a visit to a nearby winery or coffee at a local business, the Heads encourages you to slow down and appreciate your surroundings.

Despite being within easy reach of Berry and Nowra, Shoalhaven Heads maintains its own distinct village identity.

What does the property market look like?

With 57 properties sold over the rolling 12 months from October 2025 to September 2026, Shoalhaven Heads recorded a median property price of $1.1 million and 0% annual growth.

There are 35 properties currently on the market, from relocatable homes starting at $275,000 to premium property last advertised at $3.55 million, now listed as Contact Agent. Recorded sales ranged from $210,000 for a relocatable home to $1,875,000.

What selling times tell us

The median time on market was 148 days, with selling periods ranging from 16 to 998 days. These figures demonstrate how differently individual properties can perform within the same suburb.

Daniel's take

Every property has its own story, audience and position within the market. A stable median does not mean every property has held its value, and relocatable homes should not be treated as directly comparable with conventional residential property. Pricing, presentation and relevant comparable sales matter.

Beyond the numbers, Shoalhaven Heads offers a lifestyle shaped by the river, the ocean and the community that calls it home.

Source: supplied Pricefinder figures, rolling 12 months October 2025 to September 2026; current listings as at 25 November 2026. The upper asking price is the last advertised price, not a confirmed current guide. Figures include relocatable homes and are not an individual property valuation.
