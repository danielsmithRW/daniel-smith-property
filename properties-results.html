THE CAMPAIGN

Thirty-five days. One result. A new chapter on both sides.

The property came to market in Worrigee with a clear job to do: position it properly, stay close to buyer feedback and keep the campaign moving.

It sold for $785,000 after 35 days on the market.

But the part I like about this result isn't only the number. The buyers were a family purchasing their first home while also becoming first-time parents — a pretty significant new chapter all at once.

“Stories sell. Results sell. The best campaigns have both.”
